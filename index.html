📖 How to use this — and what every number means

The 3-step routine

1. Pick market + bucket. Read the four headline tiles: breakeven (never operate here), healthy & strong scale targets, and contribution per order.
2. Type your live Meta CPA into the Live CPA zone — it tells you Scale / Hold / Pull back.
3. Use Budget allocation to choose where to add spend, Promo before a sale, Sensitivity to see what to watch, and the order→profit + trajectory views for the business picture.

🎽
Shirts are your LTV play — LTV A$168/customer vs Ties A$132

Validated on 12 months of Shopify: shirt buyers return 57% of the time vs 30% for ties — 1.6× stickier, worth 2.33 orders/customer vs 1.43. So even though shirts break even at a lower first-order CPA (A$72 vs A$92), their lifetime value is +28% higher. Play it two ways: Ties = efficient cash acquisition (fast payback), Shirts = pay up to acquire and bank the repeats.

📎 How LTV is built: first-order contribution × orders-per-customer (from the real returning rate, 1÷(1−rate)). Conservative — it ignores that returning customers spend +15.5% more, so true LTV is a little higher. Tune the repeat rate & new-share in the sidebar's Cohort & LTV panel. 📏 Three rules that keep this simple:
1  Steer by Shopify-true ROAS + blended MER — never Meta-reported numbers alone.
2  Scale +15–20% every 2–3 days while 7-day ROAS holds the healthy line. Below breakeven for 3 days → cut, don't average down.
3  A dollar of CPA saved = a dollar of contribution — but a dollar of AOV added ≈ 90c of contribution. Push bundles & AOV as hard as CPA.

✅ Weekly operating checklist

Run this every week so the calculator is used the same way no matter who's behind the ads.

  • Judge on the 7-day rolling window, new-customer campaigns only.
  • Reconcile Meta spend to Shopify net sales and check blended MER ≤ 30% — you steer by Shopify/MER, not Meta-reported ROAS.
  • Green (CPA ≤ healthy): raise budget +15–20% every 2–3 days while the 7-day holds.
  • Amber (healthy → breakeven): hold, no increases.
  • Red (above breakeven CPA): pull back.
  • Put new budget where Budget allocation ranks highest, while each cell holds its target.
  • Monthly: refresh AOV, reversal %, routing %, FX and COGS if they've moved; re-confirm corporate overhead.
